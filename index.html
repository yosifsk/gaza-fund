Help Gaza Family Rebuild Life

Our Story

I am a computer systems engineer from Gaza. My family’s life changed completely due to the war. We lost our home and were forced into displacement after everything we built was destroyed. My family is known for its long history of craftsmanship and work in confectionery in Gaza City, but today we are struggling to meet basic needs such as food, shelter, and safety. Like many families here, we are trying to survive day by day and rebuild some form of stability after losing our home and facing extremely difficult circumstances.
